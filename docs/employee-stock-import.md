## Authentic schema contract

Three required sheets, with exact ordered headers (surrounding whitespace is normalized, duplicates rejected):

- `Award Summary` (12): Awardee ID, Last Name, First Name, Award ID, Award Date, Award Type, Awarded, Award Price, Vested, Unvested, Total Transacted, Expiration Date.
- `Vest Schedules` (9): Awardee ID, Last Name, First Name, Award ID, Award Date, Award Type, Award Price, Vest Date, Vest Shares.
- `Transactions` (67): full public generic header array is pinned in the parser/independent fixtures. Essential observations are transaction date/quantity, historical fair market value, transaction type, award reference/date/type/price, taxable spread, total taxes, shares for taxes, net shares and broker field. Tax-country/state/percentage cells are not silently interpreted as current holdings or tax paid.

Identifiers are strings (leading zeros significant), quantities may be fractional, dates are ISO strings, blank expiry is supported. Microsoft and USD are **review-labeled import-profile metadata supplied by Dan**, absent from workbook cells. The workbook supplies no reliable report/as-of date, current MSFT quote, FX rate, current custody holdings, sales ledger, cost basis or actual returns. UploadedAt is not report date. A dollar number format alone is not an explicit currency code. No provider market price is inferred from zero award price or historical vesting FMV.

## Financial meaning and reconciliation

- Awarded must reconcile to reported vested + unvested per grant; discrepancies remain warnings/needs review, never fabricated adjustments.
- `Total Transacted` is a separate source observation. In this export it reconciles to vesting transaction quantity; it is **not sold shares** and must not be subtracted from vested to infer held shares.
- Reported vested/delivered, unvested conditional and current held (owner-confirmed or unknown) are separate. Import remains useful while holdings/valuation are unknown. No conditional amount or grant summary is added to personal wealth.
- Summary, schedule and transaction rows are three views of grants, not three assets. Match private grant references, quantities/cumulative deliveries and ordered source dates. Preserve scheduled date and actual transaction date separately; weekend/business-day shifts must reconcile without exact-date-only matching. Passing calendar time never turns a scheduled row into an actual delivery.
- Show transaction-only delivered events and unmatched conditional schedule dates in separate chart series/table rows; summary totals remain source totals. Ambiguous, unsupported or partial reconciliation is visible. Historical FMV and tax observations remain dated source observations, not current market price, cost basis or proof of tax paid.
- USD/DKK illustrations require explicit price/rate units and separate sources/dates; no default quote or FX rate. The optional fixed MSFT research quote uses the existing Saxo LIVE market route, independently of the trading selector, and validates symbol/asset/currency/status/freshness. It shows observed/retrieved timestamps and reported or unknown delay. It changes only the viewing illustration, never the approval form or holdings. Quote/sign-in/history failures leave quantities/import available. No position-derived FX, new FX lookup, Yahoo or new paid integration is added; DKK conversion uses a reviewed manual DKK-per-USD rate. Converted/conditional values are illustrative at that stated price, not guaranteed future price, net proceeds or return. Unknown is `—`.

## Retained API and approval

All retained routes require the authenticated owner in Live and return private/no-store responses. Demo, anonymous and forged workspace requests cannot retrieve or mutate private receipts, snapshots or originals. The existing session/CSRF boundary applies to writes.

- `POST /api/employee-stocks/uploads` retains one protected original content record and a new receipt before extraction. Repeated content keeps another receipt, not another copy of the original. Unsupported parsing retains the receipt/original without accepting financial values.
- `GET /uploads`, `GET /uploads/{id}` and `GET /snapshots/{id}` provide minimized history/review DTOs. A receipt separates uploaded, needs-review, unsupported, rejected and approved states. Source date remains unknown until a separate owner-confirmed report date is approved.
- `POST /uploads/{id}/parse` retries an unfinished receipt under the same limits and version check. Approved/rejected receipts cannot be reparsed or changed.
- `POST /uploads/{id}/approve` requires current receipt version, explicit profile/date, warning acknowledgement and save confirmation. Current holdings may remain unknown; a non-null declaration, including zero, needs its own date/confirmation. Source facts are retained observations; review edits declarations, valuation and mapping rather than rewriting extracted rows.
- Identical content/review reuses its canonical snapshot. `POST /uploads/{id}/link` explicitly links another receipt to an existing approval for that original. A different review on the same report date requires explicit prior-snapshot comparison/supersession. Prior snapshots/originals remain immutable; charts use canonical approved dates without invented intermediate values or returns. A changed source identity cannot replace the existing history.
- `POST /uploads/{id}/reject` retains the original without approval. `GET /uploads/{id}/original` is an authenticated attachment, no-store, sandboxed and unavailable to AI/resource DTOs. Stored document hashes, identity hashes, protected bytes, raw names and raw award IDs are absent from ordinary DTOs.

Current wealth updates are opt-in, versioned and atomic with approval. They require a known dated current quantity, applicable dated price/FX, an explicit single-product mapping and duplicate review. Zero needs no fabricated quote. Existing possible Microsoft/MSFT records require mapping instead of duplicate creation; managed products are reviewed through their upload history, not overwritten by generic manual editing. An older report cannot change current wealth. Neither source unvested totals nor historical gross/net deliveries are used as current quantity. Other cash, pension, manual wealth and Saxo records remain outside the import.

Approved high-level advisor coverage includes only the current report date, declared current quantity/date, conditional unvested quantity and archive semantics. It excludes originals, identities, raw rows, storage metadata and unapproved previews. No paid AI call is required for validation.

## Parser and test boundaries

Use .NET ZIP/XML only; no dependency or workbook execution engine. Reject formulas/cached formulas, macros/binary parts, external relationships/links, DTD/entities/DDE, duplicate/traversal entries, unsupported/malformed cell types and duplicate/missing headers.
