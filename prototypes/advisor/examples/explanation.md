# A clearer view of your monthly room

This example uses a **synthetic family of four**. It explains the same fixture used by the interactive analyses, rather than your connected accounts.

## What is available to spend?

- Monthly income: **54,000 DKK**
- Monthly expenses: **32,700 DKK**
- Planned savings: **9,300 DKK**
- Available to spend: **12,000 DKK**

The calculation is `54,000 − 32,700 − 9,300 = 12,000 DKK`.

## What changes with another 2,000 DKK saved?

Monthly savings rise to **11,300 DKK**, leaving **10,000 DKK** available. Your income and expenses stay unchanged.

For the summer-house scenario, the target is **400,000 DKK** and the starting balance is **180,000 DKK**. There is **220,000 DKK** left to save.

- Current contribution of 5,000 DKK: **44 months** to the target.
- Contribution of 7,000 DKK: **32 months** to the target.
- Difference: **12 months sooner**.

The last contribution is capped at the amount still needed. With a first contribution in October 2026, the dates are May 2030 and May 2029 respectively.

> This is a scenario, not a change to a goal, budget or bank instruction.

## What this leaves out

Interest, investment returns, property price changes, purchase costs, taxes and withdrawals are excluded. A future advisor should explain uncertainty before turning a what-if calculation into a recommendation.
